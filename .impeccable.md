## Design Context

### Users

Primary user: Maurizio, using Lume as a personal companion for an Xteink X3.
Contexts: morning priorities, distraction-free reading, quick workout/set logging,
and an always-useful desk dashboard. The phone configures and synchronizes; the
e-ink device remains the calm glanceable surface.

### Brand Personality

**Calm, luminous, deliberate.** Lume should feel personal and well-made rather
than like a productivity SaaS. It guides attention without demanding it. Copy is
plain, concise Italian; technical detail appears only when it helps recover or
understand a connection.

### Aesthetic Direction

Native iOS, light-first, inspired by the observed Flowe app's polish and motion
without copying its identity. Warm paper neutrals, near-black ink, and one
restrained amber light accent. Editorial spacing and strong typographic contrast;
no dashboard-card grid, neon, glassmorphism, gradients, or generic rounded-card
stack. Dark mode remains supported through semantic colors but is not the primary
art direction. Motion explains connection/sync state and honors Reduce Motion.

### Design Principles

1. **Phone as instrument, X3 as paper:** configuration and edits on iPhone;
   current truth and focus on ink.
2. **One clear action per state:** scan, connect, edit, or sync—never a wall of
   equal buttons.
3. **Visible protocol truth:** distinguish searching, connecting, bonded/ready,
   syncing, and offline without exposing Bluetooth jargon by default.
4. **Italian from the first screen:** strings, 24-hour time, °C, VoiceOver labels,
   Dynamic Type, and generous hit targets are baseline behavior.
5. **Quiet confidence:** no decorative metrics or fake activity; empty states
   teach the next real action.
